Your safety comes first

Horse riding carries inherent risk. Our guides make the final call on whether a ride goes ahead and on each rider's suitability.

Eligibility

  • Minimum ages are shown on each experience. Children ride with appropriate supervision as agreed during review.
  • Rider weight and height limits depend on the horses available and are confirmed for each booking.
  • Please tell us honestly about riding experience, medical conditions and pregnancy.
  • Riders who appear to be under the influence of alcohol or drugs will not ride.

On the day

  • Wear closed shoes and long trousers. Helmets are provided and must be worn.
  • Follow the guide's instructions at all times.
  • Online weather information never replaces the guide's on-site safety assessment of wind, surf, tide and beach access.

{% elif page == 'terms' %}

Booking terms

  1. Submitting a booking request or event enquiry does not reserve a place and no payment is taken.
  2. Our team reviews each request and may approve it, decline it, ask for more information or propose changes.
  3. An approved booking shows the final price, any deposit, and a payment deadline. The place is held provisionally until that deadline.
  4. A booking is confirmed only once payment has been verified by our payment provider (or verified by us for EFT/cash).
  5. If payment isn't received by the deadline, the provisional booking is released.
  6. Prices are in South African rand (ZAR) unless stated otherwise.
  7. Rides may be paused, moved or cancelled for safety, weather or animal-welfare reasons — see the cancellation policy.
